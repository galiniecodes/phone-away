Local · YOLO26 + hands

Pretrained YOLO26 detects phones and draws yellow boxes. MediaPipe hand tracking estimates whether a phone is held. No custom training or API key is needed. Frames stay in your browser. A box means a phone is visible; the alert waits for sustained phone use. Hidden phones and hands can be missed.

Check speed

Local detection targets 10, 5, or 2 checks per second. A check must finish before the next begins, so actual speed depends on your device and model. Each check uses the current camera frame.

No frames are saved by this app. No microphone access is required. Detection identifies visible phone use; it cannot determine whether the phone is used for work.
